# Knowledge Base - Frequently Asked Questions

## Account Management

### Q: How do I reset my password?
**A:** To reset your password:
1. Go to the login page and click "Forgot Password"
2. Enter your email address
3. Check your email for a reset link (may take up to 5 minutes)
4. Click the link and create a new password
5. Your new password must be at least 8 characters with uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols

If you don't receive the email, check your spam folder or contact support.

### Q: How do I update my account information?
**A:** To update your account details:
1. Log into your account
2. Navigate to "Account Settings" or "Profile"
3. Click "Edit" next to the information you want to change
4. Make your changes and click "Save"
5. You'll receive a confirmation email for security purposes

### Q: Why is my account suspended?
**A:** Account suspensions typically occur due to:
- Multiple failed login attempts
- Suspicious activity detected
- Outstanding payment issues
- Terms of service violations
- Security precautions

Contact our support team immediately to resolve suspension issues.

### Q: How do I cancel my subscription?
**A:** To cancel your subscription:
1. Log into your account
2. Go to "Billing" or "Subscription Settings"
3. Click "Cancel Subscription"
4. Follow the confirmation steps
5. You'll receive a cancellation confirmation email

Your service will continue until the end of your current billing period.

## Billing and Payments

### Q: Why was my payment declined?
**A:** Payment declines can happen due to:
- Insufficient funds in your account
- Expired credit card
- Incorrect billing information
- Bank security measures
- Credit limit exceeded

Please verify your payment information or try a different payment method.

### Q: How do I update my payment method?
**A:** To update your payment information:
1. Log into your account
2. Navigate to "Billing" or "Payment Methods"
3. Click "Add Payment Method" or "Edit" existing method
4. Enter your new payment details
5. Click "Save" to confirm changes

### Q: Can I get a refund?
**A:** Refund eligibility depends on:
- Time since purchase (within 30 days for most products)
- Product type and usage
- Reason for refund request
- Terms of service agreement

Contact support with your order details for refund evaluation.

### Q: Where can I find my invoices?
**A:** To access your invoices:
1. Log into your account
2. Go to "Billing" or "Account"
3. Click "Invoice History" or "Billing History"
4. Download or print invoices as needed

Invoices are also emailed to your registered email address.
