## Refund and Cancellation Policy

### Refund Eligibility
We want you to be completely satisfied with our services. Our refund policy provides fair protection for both customers and our business.

**Eligible for Full Refund:**
- Cancellation within 30 days of initial purchase
- Technical issues that cannot be resolved
- Service outages exceeding SLA commitments
- Billing errors or unauthorized charges

**Partial Refund Scenarios:**
- Pro-rated refunds for annual subscriptions
- Downgrade to lower service tier
- Temporary service suspensions

**Not Eligible for Refund:**
- Services used beyond 30-day period
- Customization or setup fees
- Third-party integrations or add-ons
- Policy violations resulting in termination

### Cancellation Process
**Self-Service Cancellation:**
1. Log into your account dashboard
2. Navigate to "Billing & Subscriptions"
3. Click "Cancel Subscription"
4. Follow confirmation prompts
5. Receive cancellation confirmation email

**Assisted Cancellation:**
- Contact support via live chat, email, or phone
- Provide account details and reason for cancellation
- Support team will process cancellation within 24 hours
- Confirmation sent via email

### Refund Processing Times
- **Credit Card Refunds**: 5-10 business days
- **PayPal Refunds**: 3-5 business days
- **Bank Transfers**: 7-14 business days
- **Check Payments**: 2-3 weeks

## Service Level Agreement (SLA)

### Uptime Commitments
We guarantee the following minimum uptime percentages for our services:

**Service Availability:**
- **Standard Plan**: 99.0% uptime (7.2 hours downtime/month max)
- **Professional Plan**: 99.5% uptime (3.6 hours downtime/month max)
- **Enterprise Plan**: 99.9% uptime (43 minutes downtime/month max)

**Planned Maintenance:**
- Scheduled during low-usage periods
- 48-hour advance notice for major maintenance
- Maximum 4 hours per month for planned downtime
- Emergency maintenance may occur without notice

### Performance Standards
**Response Times:**
- API response time: < 500ms for 95% of requests
- Dashboard loading: < 3 seconds initial load
- File sync operations: < 30 seconds for files under 100MB
- Search queries: < 2 seconds for typical datasets

**Support Response Times:**
- **Priority 1 (Critical)**: 1 hour response, 4 hours resolution
- **Priority 2 (High)**: 4 hours response, 24 hours resolution
- **Priority 3 (Medium)**: 24 hours response, 72 hours resolution
- **Priority 4 (Low)**: 72 hours response, 1 week resolution

### SLA Credits
If we fail to meet our SLA commitments, you may be eligible for service credits:

**Uptime Credits:**
- 99.0% - 98.0%: 5% monthly service credit
- 98.0% - 95.0%: 15% monthly service credit  
- Below 95.0%: 25% monthly service credit

**Support Credits:**
- Response time exceeded by 2x: 10% monthly credit
- Response time exceeded by 4x: 25% monthly credit

**How to Request Credits:**
1. Submit SLA credit request within 30 days
2. Provide incident details and timestamps
3. We will verify the claim and process credits
4. Credits applied to next billing cycle

Our services are designed to support legitimate business and personal use cases:

**Allowed Activities:**
- Business data storage and collaboration
- Personal file backup and synchronization
- Development and testing environments
- Educational and research projects
- Creative content creation and sharing

### Prohibited Uses
**Strictly Forbidden:**
- Illegal activities or content distribution
- Copyright infringement or piracy
- Malware, viruses, or malicious code
- Spam or unsolicited bulk communications

**Resource Abuse:**
- Excessive bandwidth consumption
- Running background services unrelated to our platform
- Reselling or sublicensing services without authorization

1. **First Violation**: Written warning and guidance
2. **Second Violation**: Temporary service suspension
3. **Third Violation**: Account termination

1. Submit an appeal within 30 days
2. Provide detailed explanation and evidence

4. Decision will be communicated via email
5. Further appeals can be made to senior management

## Security and Compliance

### Security Measures
We implement industry-leading security practices to protect your data:

**Encryption:**
- Data in transit: TLS 1.3 encryption
- Data at rest: AES-256 encryption
- Key management: Hardware security modules (HSMs)
- Zero-knowledge architecture for sensitive data

**Access Controls:**
- Multi-factor authentication required
- Role-based access permissions
- Regular access reviews and audits
- Privileged access management (PAM)

**Infrastructure Security:**
- 24/7 security monitoring and incident response
- Regular penetration testing and vulnerability assessments
- Secure software development lifecycle (SSDLC)
- Physical security at data center facilities

### Compliance Certifications
**Current Certifications:**
- SOC 2 Type II (Security, Availability, Confidentiality)
- ISO 27001 Information Security Management
- GDPR Compliance (EU General Data Protection Regulation)
- HIPAA Compliance (Healthcare data protection)
- PCI DSS Level 1 (Payment card industry standards)

**Industry Standards:**
- NIST Cybersecurity Framework alignment
- Cloud Security Alliance (CSA) best practices
- OWASP secure coding standards
- Regular third-party security audits

### Incident Response
**Security Incident Procedure:**
1. **Detection**: Automated monitoring and manual reporting
2. **Assessment**: Rapid triage and impact evaluation
3. **Containment**: Immediate steps to limit damage
4. **Investigation**: Forensic analysis and root cause determination
5. **Communication**: Timely notification to affected customers
6. **Resolution**: Complete remediation and service restoration
7. **Post-Incident Review**: Process improvements and lessons learned

**Customer Notification:**
- Critical incidents: Within 1 hour of detection
- Significant incidents: Within 4 hours of detection
- Minor incidents: Within 24 hours of detection
- Data breach notifications as required by applicable laws

### Business Continuity
**Disaster Recovery:**
- Recovery Time Objective (RTO): 4 hours maximum
- Recovery Point Objective (RPO): 1 hour maximum data loss
- Multiple geographically distributed data centers
- Regular disaster recovery testing and drills

**Data Backup:**
- Automated daily backups with point-in-time recovery
- Geographic redundancy across multiple regions
- 90-day backup retention for standard plans
- Extended retention available for enterprise customers
